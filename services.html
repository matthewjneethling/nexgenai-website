Services

Start with a clear plan. Then build what pays off.

Every service is separate, scoped in writing and agreed before any work starts. You can stop after any step.

Step 1

AI Audit

Start here

I look at how your business runs, find where time is being wasted, and give you a clear plan of which AI tools fix it and what they'll save you.

  • Short intake form, my own research on your business, then a 60-minute audit call
  • AI Opportunities Report: up to 5 tools, what each fixes, monthly cost and estimated hours saved
  • A 30-minute walkthrough of the report, with direct links to every tool
Step 2

Implementation

The audit tells you what to use. Implementation is me setting it up properly and getting your team using it, so you see the savings in weeks, not months.

  • Setup, settings and templates built around how your business works
  • Connected to what you already use: email, calendar, WhatsApp Business, spreadsheets
  • Tested with real examples, plus team training and a clear handover
Step 3

Monthly Service Check

Every month I check and service everything I've set up, fix anything that's off and keep improving it, so the time savings keep coming.

  • Health check of every tool and automation, with fixes and updates
  • New staff shown how the tools work
  • A short monthly report with one or two recommended improvements. Cancel any time
Anytime

Support & call-outs

If something needs fixing or your team needs help outside the monthly check, I can jump on remotely or come out to you.

  • Remote help by phone, video call or logging into your systems
  • On-site visits anywhere in South Africa
  • Billed by the hour, with an estimate agreed before I start
Later

Future projects

Once the first tools are working, we can look at the next area of the business.

Anything custom-built is done with a vetted builder partner. I manage the project and stay your single point of contact.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. Everything is explained in plain language, and if you choose implementation, your team is trained on every tool.

Will you just try to sell me software?

No. I'm not tied to any tool. If a simple, cheap option does the job, that's what I'll recommend. If AI isn't the right fix, I'll tell you.

Do I have to buy implementation after the audit?

No. The report gives you direct links so you can set things up yourself. Implementation is a separate choice, quoted in writing.

What if I change my mind before the audit?

If you cancel before the audit starts, you get your money back in full.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different, so there's no one-size-fits-all price. We start with a 15-minute discovery call, then I look into your business and how your work flows. Once I can see what fits, I'll give you a clear quote based on your business.

Curious where your business is losing time?

Start with a 15-minute discovery call. You'll leave knowing whether an AI Audit makes sense for you, with no pressure either way.
