About

Consulting first. Technology second.

NexGen AI exists to help business owners spend less time on repetitive work and more time on the work that grows the business.

Founder

Hi, I'm Matthew.

I'm the founder of NexGen AI, based in Cape Town. I help businesses find where AI and automation can genuinely save time, cut costs and free the business up to make more money, and where they can't.

Most owners I speak to know AI matters, but don't know where to start or who to trust. There are thousands of tools and plenty of hype. My job is to cut through that: understand your business first, then recommend only what fits.

I learned from the best. I trained through the AAA Accelerator, the AI consulting and automation program and mentorship founded by Liam Ottley, who also founded the AI agency Morningside AI and is one of the most followed voices in AI business today. Through the full program I built the skills to audit a business properly, choose the right tools and deliver more than expected for every client.

I also run my own business on AI systems every day, so everything I recommend is something I use myself. When you work with me, you deal with me directly, from the first call to the final handover.
