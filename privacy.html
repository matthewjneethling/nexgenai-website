Privacy policy

How we handle your personal information, in plain English.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Who we are

NexGen AI ("we", "us") is an AI consulting business based in Cape Town, South Africa, run by Matthew Neethling. We are the responsible party for the personal information described on this page, and Matthew is our information officer. Contact us at matthewjneethling@gmail.com or 082 416 1970.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us:

  • Your name, email address and phone number, when you book a call, email us, call us or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Details about your business and its challenges, when you answer the questions on our booking form or share information in a call.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to you and arrange calls.
  • To prepare properly for your discovery call and understand your business.
  • To provide our services if you decide to work with us, and to send agreements and invoices.

Who handles your information

We use trusted tools to run the business, including Calendly (bookings), Google Workspace (email, calendar and documents), WhatsApp and Zoom or Google Meet (calls). These providers process information on our behalf under their own privacy policies. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else without your permission.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the booking calendar is provided by Calendly, which may use cookies when you use it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the reasons above, or as the law requires, and then delete it.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email matthewjneethling@gmail.com and we will respond promptly. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can also contact the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
